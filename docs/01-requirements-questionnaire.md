# Client Requirements Questionnaire
Tip: ask him to show his current register/Excel/WhatsApp flow and watch a real admission + payment. Record answers below.

## Business
- Members (approx)? Branches? Timings? Trainers/staff count?
- How does someone join today, start to finish?
- What costs you the most time or money right now?

## Members
- Details collected (name, phone, DOB, address, emergency contact, photo, medical notes)?
- Old data to migrate (register / Excel)? How many records?
- Trial / guest members?

## Membership
- Plans and prices (1/3/6/12 months, personal training, couple/family)?
- Joining/admission fee? Discounts? Freezing/pausing? Grace period after expiry?

## Payments
- Cash / UPI / card today? Partial payments and dues?
- Want online payment? Razorpay account exists (needs his business KYC)?
- GST invoices needed? (confirm with his CA)

## Attendance
- Needed at all? Why? Register / QR / biometric?

## Trainers
- How many? Assigned to members? Salary or commission? Schedules?

## Reports
- What do you check daily / monthly? (collection, dues, expiring, new joins)

## Notifications
- Who reminds members today? WhatsApp / SMS / call? Languages (English/Hindi)?

## Website
- Pages wanted? Photos/videos available? Reference sites he likes?

## Branding / Domain / Hosting
- Logo, colours, Instagram, Google Business Profile? Domain owned? Who pays hosting?

## Future
- 2nd branch? Mobile app? Online coaching? Supplements/merch?

## Process
- Who approves decisions? Who uses the admin panel daily? Budget range? Deadline?
